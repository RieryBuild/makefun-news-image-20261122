## The 13 panels

### Heroes

1. **COP31 goes to a Sunday vote — Antalya, Türkiye.**
   The COP31 presidency tabled its final package at ~02:00 local time
   on Sunday 22 Nov (23:00 GMT Sat 21 Nov). President Murat Kurum:
   "a text which all the parties can agree upon". Objections were due
   03:00 GMT; the approval session was set for 06:00 GMT, later said
   to aim for 10:00–10:30 local (07:00–07:30 GMT). The text omits
   fossil fuels entirely; it keeps the ambition to raise electricity's
   share of final energy to 35% by 2035 (from ~23%), adds a
   gigatonne-scale CCUS goal by 2035, and drops the $120bn/yr-by-2035
   adaptation-finance figure in favour of the Antalya-to-Addis Ababa
   Roadmap. As of the build (~07:00 UTC 22 Nov) NO adopted deal had
   been confirmed — the panel says "goes to a vote", not "adopted".
   Sources: Reuters (22 Nov), AFP, CarrZee.
2. **Verstappen wins a Las Vegas thriller — Las Vegas, USA.**
   Max Verstappen won the Las Vegas Grand Prix on Saturday night
   local time (result ~04:00 UTC Sun 22 Nov), 1:27:39.624, ahead of
   Lando Norris (+0.639) and Oscar Piastri (+1.529). 75th career win,
   4th of the season, 3rd Vegas win in four years; 35 lead changes.
   Antonelli 4th, Gasly 5th; Russell hit the barriers three laps from
   the end and finished 9th; the Ferraris collided.
   Sources: Reuters, Formula1.com, AP (22 Nov).
3. **India post a record 720/9 — Wellington, New Zealand.**
   Day 4, Sunday 22 Nov: India declared on 720/9, the highest Test
   total ever in New Zealand (previous best 715/6 dec, 2019), a lead
   of 352 over New Zealand's 368. Rishabh Pant 128 (9th Test hundred;
   108 career sixes, past McCullum's 107), KL Rahul 136, Shubman Gill
   98, Yashasvi Jaiswal 85, Devdutt Padikkal 80, Dhruv Jurel 78 — the
   first time all of India's first seven partnerships reached 50. New
   Zealand closed on 119/2 (Conway 59*, Ravindra 27*), trailing by
   233. Source: ESPNcricinfo (22 Nov).

### Panels 4–13

4. **Nepal's PM lands in Delhi — New Delhi, India.** Prime Minister
   Balendra Shah arrived in Delhi on Sunday 22 Nov (departs Kathmandu
   13:45, arrives 15:45), meets External Affairs Minister Jaishankar
   at 18:00; talks with Prime Minister Modi follow on Monday 23 Nov
   at Hyderabad House. 34-member delegation incl. Finance Minister
   Swarnim Wagle and Foreign Minister Shishir Khanal; first bilateral
   trip since taking office in March 2026; returns Monday 18:35.
   Sources: IANS/ANI, Nepal MFA (22 Nov).
5. **Hong Kong picks its electors — Hong Kong.** Polls opened 09:00
   local for the Election Committee: 856 candidates for 723 seats
   across 31 subsectors, 5,757 eligible voters, 259 returned
   uncontested. The 1,500-member committee picks the next Chief
   Executive (2027) and 40 of 90 LegCo seats; term from 1 Feb 2027.
   Results come after polls close (18:00 HKT = 10:00 UTC) — the panel
   covers voting under way. Sources: China Daily Asia, news.gov.hk
   (22 Nov).
6. **M4.1 quake shakes Sagaing — Myanmar.** Sunday 22 Nov 02:35:57
   IST (03:35 Yangon; 21:05 UTC 21 Nov), magnitude 4.1, depth 95 km,
   37 km north-east of Tamu, Sagaing (24.404 N, 94.616 E). No damage
   reports by morning. Source: India's National Centre for
   Seismology, via ANI (22 Nov).
7. **Hamas seeks urgent talks — Gaza.** Hamas called for urgent talks
   after an Israeli strike killed three soup-kitchen/aid workers in
   Tel al-Hawa (strike Saturday 21 Nov midday; the call was published
   Sunday 22 Nov 05:11 UTC). Three more Palestinians were killed in
   strikes on Sunday (drone north-west of Rafah, a fisherman, a
   15-year-old boy south of Khan Younis), per Anadolu. The Gaza Health
   Ministry's toll since the October 2025 ceasefire is reported as
   1,662–1,671 killed, varying by source and date. Source: Anadolu
   (22 Nov). Note: weaker mirrors report 7 killed on Sunday; the
   panel uses Anadolu's 3.
8. **Swimmer crosses Hormuz — Strait of Hormuz.** British endurance
   swimmer Lewis Pugh, UN Patron of the Oceans, completed a three-day,
   ~15 km staged swim across the Strait of Hormuz (Musandam to the
   Cape of Hormuz, ~6.5 hours of swimming), first reported by AP on
   Sunday 22 Nov 04:31 UTC. Thirty minutes into day one, an oil
   tanker was hit ~4 km away; he passed a burning gas carrier with
   flames, fireballs and a chemical smell. First long-distance swim
   there since the war began. Source: AP (22 Nov). (The swim itself
   ran Mon–Wed of that week; it is included under the "first reported
   on 22 Nov" clause.)
9. **Zverev meets Alcaraz for the title — Turin, Italy. FORECAST.**
   ATP Finals final, Sunday 22 Nov, not before 18:00 CET (17:00 UTC).
   World No. 1 Alexander Zverev (69 wins this season, no set dropped)
   vs Carlos Alcaraz (13-match win streak; leads their head-to-head
   7–6). Undefeated champion takes $5,171,000; the loser $2,759,000.
   Source: ATP Tour (22 Nov).
10. **Pochettino's debut at Anfield — Liverpool, England. FORECAST.**
    Liverpool v Manchester United, Sunday 22 Nov 16:30 GMT, Anfield.
    Mauricio Pochettino takes charge of United for the first time
    (replacing Michael Carrick, sacked after the 4–4 draw at Aston
    Villa on 8 Nov). United sit 18th, one win in ten, their worst
    start in 40 years; Liverpool are 4th. Source: TNT Sports (22 Nov).
11. **40,000 run Philadelphia — Philadelphia, USA. FORECAST.**
    Philadelphia Marathon, Sunday 22 Nov, starts 07:00 ET; a record
    40,000-participant weekend (20,000 in the marathon itself).
12. **Saudi defences down Houthi missile — Saudi Arabia.** The
    Saudi-led coalition intercepted a Houthi ballistic missile aimed
    at the west of the kingdom in the early hours of Sunday 22 Nov
    (~01:42 local; civil-defence alert for the Mecca region ~23:43
    Sat). The coalition also said three Houthi missiles aimed at
    Riyadh were stopped. Source: Anadolu (22 Nov). (Bahrain's
    Iranian-attack industrial-site fire was Saturday 21 Nov and is
    NOT folded into this item.)
13. **A gamer's tip freed a missing girl — Oregon, USA.** The BBC
    tells the story today (feature published Sunday 22 Nov): Colorado
    teenager Landon Tresp, 16, met a missing 17-year-old girl
    ("Jenny") in the game Among Us, told his mother Khristina on
    31 Oct; the FBI monitored a call and rescued the girl on 1 Nov
    from a basement in Huntington, Oregon — she had been missing since
    May 2025, aged 15. Suspect Adam Hunter Harmel, 39, is in federal
    custody. Source: BBC (22 Nov). (Rescue 1 Nov; included under the
    "first reported on 22 Nov" clause.)
