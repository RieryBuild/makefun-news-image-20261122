## The 13 panels

### Heroes

1. **COP31 collapses with no deal at all — Antalya, Türkiye.**
   At the closing plenary on Sunday, COP31 President Murat Kurum said
   "It is clear that there are parties in this hall who oppose this
   text. We recognise that there is no consensus on this document.
   Therefore, these decisions have not been adopted." The EU and others
   rejected the final draft, which never mentions fossil fuels. The
   text kept the 35%-by-2035 electricity-share ambition and a
   gigatonne-scale CCUS goal, and dropped the $120bn/yr-by-2035
   adaptation-finance figure. The last COP to end with no deal adopted
   by all was Copenhagen in 2009. Sources: Reuters (22 Nov),
   BusinessDay (22 Nov). (Round 2 said "goes to a vote" — the vote
   never produced a deal; this is the freshest hero in the field.)
2. **Russia fires 186 drones; 161 downed — Kyiv, Ukraine.**
   The overnight raid ran from 18:00 Saturday 21 Nov; by 08:00 Kyiv
   time Sunday the Ukrainian Air Force reported 161 of 186 drones shot
   down or suppressed, including 56 of 72 jet-powered Shaheds. Strikes
   were recorded at 18 locations, debris at six more. A five-storey
   block in Dymer was destroyed; nine residents were evacuated and
   President Zelenskyy later said 14 people were unaccounted for.
   Sources: Ukrainian Air Force / General Staff via myukraineis.org,
   h.ua, Reuters wires (22 Nov). CORRECTION vs the field: #5's
   "148 drones, 3 missiles" is wrong — the Air Force's figure is 186
   drones, no missiles in this raid.
3. **Hong Kong elects its electors in record numbers — Hong Kong.**
   Final turnout 98.79% — 5,640 of 5,709 voters — a record, beating
   89.77% in 2021. 856 candidates contested 723 seats across 31
   subsectors; polls ran 09:00–18:00 HKT. The 1,500-member committee
   picks the next Chief Executive (2027) and 40 of 90 LegCo seats.
   Counting ran through the evening. Sources: info.gov.hk (22 Nov),
   China Daily Asia (22 Nov). CORRECTION vs the field: 44.7% (10:00)
   and 93.55% (14:00) were mid-day figures, superseded by the final
   98.79%.

### Panels 4–13

4. **Iran claims two US radars hit; Bahrain denies it — Manama, Bahrain.**
   CLAIM. The IRGC said late Saturday night that missiles and drones
   hit two US Patriot radars at al-Riffa and Awali Sakhir. Bahrain's
   defence ministry said on Sunday its defences intercepted the attack;
   falling debris caused light damage, no injuries. The IRGC posted
   launch video on Sunday morning. No US statement by press time.
   The strikes happened late Saturday; counted as Sunday's news
   because the claim and Bahrain's account reached the wires early
   Sunday, with fresh updates Sunday morning. Sources: Xinhua
   (21 Nov statement), ABNA (22 Nov 04:46 UTC), israel.com live
   updates (22 Nov 07:07/08:07 Israel time), Al Jazeera.
   (The IRGC's separate claim of downing a US MQ-9 and an MQ-1 over
   Qeshm/Hormuz dates to Saturday 21 Nov and is NOT a story of its
   own; it is context for the Bahrain claim only.)
5. **Russian cargo ship Sparta II still burning — Gulf of Finland.**
   Hit by two drones on Saturday morning, the ship drifts in Finnish
   waters, still ablaze on Sunday; Finnish and Estonian vessels plus a
   Russian rescue ship fight the fire. All 16 crew were evacuated to a
   Russian vessel. Fire crews warn she could sink as firefighting
   water builds up in the hull. A Russian helicopter is suspected of
   entering Finnish airspace (4.1 km, Sat 12:34) during the rescue.
   Finland's military says Ukraine was likely behind the strike.
   Sources: Reuters (22 Nov), Helsinki Times (22 Nov).
   (Hit Saturday, still burning Sunday — stated on-panel.)
6. **India pile up a record 720/9 in New Zealand — Wellington.**
   Day 4, Sunday 22 Nov: India declared on 720/9, the highest Test
   total ever in New Zealand. Rishabh Pant 128, KL Rahul 136, Shubman
   Gill 98. New Zealand closed on 119/2, trailing by 233.
   Source: ESPNcricinfo (22 Nov).
7. **Verstappen takes the Las Vegas GP — Las Vegas, USA.**
   Max Verstappen won on Saturday night local time (result ~04:00 UTC
   Sun 22 Nov), 1:27:39.624, ahead of Lando Norris (+0.639) and Oscar
   Piastri (+1.529). 75th career win. Russell hit the barriers three
   laps from the end and finished 9th. Sources: Reuters,
   Formula1.com (22 Nov).
8. **Hamas seeks urgent talks after aid-van strike — Gaza City.**
   Hamas called for urgent talks on Sunday (call published 05:11 UTC)
   after an Israeli strike killed three soup-kitchen/aid workers in
   Tel al-Hawa on Saturday. Three more Palestinians were killed in
   strikes on Sunday (drone north-west of Rafah, a fisherman, a
   15-year-old boy south of Khan Younis), per Anadolu.
   Sources: Anadolu (22 Nov). (The strike was Saturday; the talks
   call is Sunday's news — stated on-panel.)
9. **Nepal's PM lands in Delhi on first visit — New Delhi, India.**
   Prime Minister Balendra Shah arrived in Delhi on Sunday 22 Nov
   (departs Kathmandu 13:45, arrives 15:45), meets External Affairs
   Minister Jaishankar at 18:00; talks with Prime Minister Modi follow
   on Monday 23 Nov at Hyderabad House. First bilateral trip since
   taking office in March 2026. Sources: IANS/ANI, Nepal MFA (22 Nov).
10. **Pope Leo lunches with 1,300 poor guests — The Vatican.**
    On the 10th World Day of the Poor (postponed a week by his Latin
    America trip), Pope Leo XIV shared roast turkey with 1,300 poor
    guests in the Paul VI Hall after the Angelus, at which he prayed
    for the victims of war and for drought-stricken Papua New Guinea.
    Rome's soup kitchens served a meal "in communion" with him.
    Sources: Vatican News, Where Peter Is (22 Nov).
11. **MSF: El-Fasher's missing still unaccounted for — El-Fasher, Sudan.**
    MSF warns the fate of hundreds of thousands who fled El-Fasher is
    unknown, one month after the RSF seized the city on 26 Oct. MSF's
    president says about 5,000 people have been observed reaching
    Tawila, of some 260,000 who were in the city in late August (UN);
    six in ten adults surveyed show signs of starvation. Yale's
    Humanitarian Research Lab found earth disturbances consistent with
    mass graves at a mosque and the former Children's Hospital.
    Sources: MSF via Arab Press (22 Nov), MSF release.
    (The MSF release also cites ~10,000 arrivals per NRC registration;
    the panel uses MSF's observed ~5,000.)
12. **Saudi defences down a missile near Mecca — Riyadh, Saudi Arabia.**
    A Houthi ballistic missile aimed at the west of the kingdom was
    intercepted and destroyed in the early hours of Sunday (~01:42
    local; civil-defence alert for the Mecca region ~23:43 Sat). A
    Riyadh-bound Houthi drone was downed later on Sunday — the fourth
    interception in 24 hours. Sources: SPA (22 Nov), AzerNews
    (22 Nov). (The three-missile Riyadh intercept was Saturday and is
    context only.)
13. **China hits back at the US carrier ban — Beijing, China.**
    China's commerce ministry said on Sunday it "firmly opposes" the
    FCC's Thursday vote barring China Mobile, China Telecom and China
    Unicom (and other IP-enabled providers) from direct access to US
    phone numbers, and warned of countermeasures. The FCC cited
    national security. Source: Reuters (22 Nov, Shanghai).
